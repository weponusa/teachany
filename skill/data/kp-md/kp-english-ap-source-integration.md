## 课标原文（AP Course Framework）

### 课标原文

Students evaluate, synthesize, and cite research to support their arguments.

Students will analyze what makes others’ arguments convincing or confusing, engaging or dull, persuasive or powerless.

Students will then turn to the act of composition themselves, seeking to emulate effective argumentation they have encountered in their reading and analysis.

The course focuses on effective reading and writing practices, emphasizing depth of knowledge over breadth.
